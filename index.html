What is AnatoX?

AnatoX is a platform where students create interactive 3D models of real-world objects and learn how their different parts work together.

Students can rotate, zoom, explore components, understand their functions and explain how the complete object works.

Main Motive

The main motive of AnatoX is to turn practical learning into verified skills.

Instead of only memorizing theory, students create something practical, explain what they have learned and prove their understanding.

Challenges & Solutions

Potential Challenge AnatoX Solution
01 Students may copy models from the internet. Use originality checks and ask students questions about their own projects.
02 AI may incorrectly evaluate a project. Use structured grading rules and human review for uncertain cases.
03 Large 3D models require significant storage. Convert models into optimized GLB files and use scalable cloud storage.
04 Large models may run slowly on weak devices. Automatically create low, medium and high-quality versions.
05 An easy certificate may lose its value. Require multiple verified projects and minimum performance standards.
06 Students may worry about ownership of their models. Students remain the owners of their work. AnatoX only stores, verifies and displays it with permission.

AnatoX Excellence Certificate

🏆 Verified Excellence Certificate

Students who successfully complete 15 verified projects can earn an AnatoX Excellence Certificate.

The certificate can include the student's overall grade, performance score and a unique verification ID.

Students can showcase the certificate and their interactive portfolio during interviews, internships and future opportunities.
